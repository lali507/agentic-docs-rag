# Requirements: Loans

## Requirement 1

A volunteer can lend a book to a verified borrower. A borrower whose phone is not verified cannot borrow.

## Requirement 2

A loan has a due date, 30 days after the lending date by default.

## Requirement 3

A borrower can hold at most 3 books at the same time.
